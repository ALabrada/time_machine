## 1.0.0

* Pure-Dart external content layer shared by the Flutter app and the proxy
  server.
* `NetworkService` registry for historical-photo providers and geocoders.
* Providers for PastVu, RePhotos, HistoryPin, SepiaTown and Russia in Photo.
* Geocoders for Geonames, OSM, VKMaps and Yandex.
* Telegram publisher that submits posts as Telegram suggested posts.
